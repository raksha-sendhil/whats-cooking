## Who I am
- A software developer who likes building things, not just reading about them.
- Comfortable with Python and web development; basic working knowledge of machine learning.

## What I want from this paper
- What changed this week in AI and developer tools, and what it actually makes possible.
- Problems many people keep running into that nobody has solved well yet.
- Ideas I could build alone or with a small team, and what I'd need to learn first.
- Techniques that work in one area and haven't been tried in another.

## How to write for me
- Plain words, short sentences. Explain names I might not know; don't explain the obvious.
- Concrete over hype: what's actually new, and what's only an announcement.
- In "For you", suggest one specific thing to build, try or learn.
- Say when something is early or unproven.

## Not interested in
- General politics, celebrity or sports news, even when it trends on tech sites.
